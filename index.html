About Our Summer School

At Talent Discovery Schools, we believe every child is a creator. Our 4‑week coding programme takes beginners from "I have an idea" all the way to "It's live on the internet!"

No experience needed – just curiosity and a willingness to try. Students build a real school website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, guided step‑by‑step.

12 Classes
1 Live Project
100% Hands‑on

What You'll Build & Learn

🌐

HTML Structure

Create headings, paragraphs, lists, and links – the skeleton of every webpage.

🎨

CSS Styling

Add colours, fonts, spacing, and layouts to make your site beautiful and responsive.

⚡

JavaScript Interactivity

Make buttons respond, menus toggle, and pages come alive with user actions.

📱

Mobile‑First Design

Ensure your site looks fantastic on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
